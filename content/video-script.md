# DealMemory: 3-Minute Demo Video Script
**Theme:** AI Agents That Learn Using Hindsight
**Target Duration:** 3:00 (Structured into 5 Acts)

---

### **[0:00 - 0:30] Act 1: The Problem & The 60-Second Hook**
- **Visual:** Screen shows rep opening Salesforce/CRM with 40+ pages of dense, messy meeting notes for "Acme Financial".
- **Voiceover:** *"It's 2 minutes before a high-stakes call with Acme's CFO. You have 5 months of notes across 6 stakeholders. What did she say about our $280k quote? Why did the VP of Engineering block us in July? If you rely on a standard CRM or a stateless chatbot, you're flying blind."*

---

### **[0:30 - 1:15] Act 2: Memory OFF vs. Memory ON Split View**
- **Visual:** Switch to DealMemory `/compare` page. Show side-by-side execution.
- **Voiceover:** *"Here is our signature feature: the Memory Split-View. On the left, a standard stateless LLM given only the latest note. It gives generic boilerplate tips and completely misses the CFO's $240k budget cap.*
*On the right, DealMemory powered by Hindsight. It instantly recalls Evelyn Vance's exact words, highlights the $240k counter-offer, proves InfoSec passed on August 28th, and generates 3 targeted questions with clickable citation chips."*

---

### **[1:15 - 1:50] Act 3: Real-Time Ingest & Memory Defense**
- **Visual:** Navigate to `/ingest`. Paste a raw messy call transcript containing an API key and a competitor pitch. Click `Execute retain()`.
- **Voiceover:** *"Watch what happens during live ingestion. DealMemory doesn't just store text — Hindsight automatically extracts entities, facts, and sentiment in real time, while our Memory Defense gateway neutralizes prompt injections and scrubs sensitive API keys before they ever touch persistent storage."*

---

### **[1:50 - 2:15] Act 4: Memory Inspector & Stakeholder Stance Reversal**
- **Visual:** Navigate to `/deals/deal-swiggy-delivery` -> `Memory Inspector` tab.
- **Voiceover:** *"Hindsight continuously updates its internal mental model of stakeholders. Look at Swiggy's VP of Engineering, Rajesh Kumar. On August 14th, he was an aggressive blocker complaining about integration complexity. But on August 22nd, after a 45-minute Kafka sandbox, Hindsight recorded his 180-degree stance reversal into our primary deal champion."*

---

### **[2:15 - 2:40] Act 5: The Learning Curve & Tactic Recommender**
- **Visual:** Navigate to `/learning-curve`, show the SVG progression from 28% to 96%, then switch to `/tactics`.
- **Voiceover:** *"Because all deals share one persistent memory bank, DealMemory compounds intelligence. Our objective benchmark shows accuracy rising from 28% to 96%. And in the Tactic Recommender, Hindsight reflects across 17 past deals to prove that offering a Phased Milestone Ramp beats ApexCloud with an 86% empirical win rate versus 28% for discounting."*

---

### **[2:40 - 3:00] Act 6: Closing & Impact**
- **Visual:** Show Microsoft Teams Adaptive Card export and one-click Follow-up Email Drafter.
- **Voiceover:** *"DealMemory turns every past deal into an unfair sales advantage. Powered by Hindsight, integrated with Microsoft Teams, and built for the modern enterprise. Thank you!"*
