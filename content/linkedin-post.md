# LinkedIn Post Draft: Launching DealMemory 🚀

Sales reps spend an average of 4.5 hours every week re-reading messy CRM notes before executive calls.

They forget:
❌ Which competitor was pitched 3 weeks ago
❌ What budget cap the CFO mandated in the pricing review
❌ Why the VP of Engineering was blocking the deal (and how their stance shifted)

Large Language Models alone don't solve this. If you give a stateless LLM just the latest CRM note, it has zero historical memory. If you dump 9 months of transcripts into a prompt, it hallucinates and suffers from recency bias.

For **Hack with Hyderabad 3.0** ("AI Agents That Learn Using Hindsight"), we built **DealMemory** — an autonomous Deal Intelligence Agent powered by the **Hindsight Memory Engine** (@Vectorize.io).

Here is what makes DealMemory fundamentally different:

1️⃣ **Persistent Temporal Memory (`retain`)**: Every discovery call, email, and security audit is retained with real-time entity extraction and memory defense sanitizing PII.
2️⃣ **TEMPR Recall (`recall`)**: Ask temporal questions like *"What did Evelyn say about our $280k pricing last month?"* and get exact quotes with clickable source citations.
3️⃣ **Auto-Consolidated Observations**: Automatically detects when a stakeholder changes their mind (e.g. Swiggy VP Eng reversing from aggressive blocker to core champion after a 45-min sandbox).
4️⃣ **Cross-Deal Learning (`reflect`)**: Learns from 17 closed accounts. Across our pipeline, countering pricing objections with a Phased Milestone Ramp yielded an **86% win-rate vs 28% for discounting** (+58% win-rate uplift).
